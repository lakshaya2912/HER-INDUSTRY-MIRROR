**“Innovation for Women: Technology-Driven Solutions for Education and Skill Development”**

---

## 🎯 What is HER-INDUSTRY-MIRROR?

**HER-INDUSTRY-MIRROR** is an AI-powered Flight Simulator & Competency Verification platform designed specifically for women preparing to enter or advance in technology careers. 

While academic degrees teach theoretical algorithms, women often drop out or feel severe imposter syndrome due to a lack of safe spaces to rehearse high-pressure real-world situations (e.g., live production crashes, sprint reviews, salary negotiations, and assertive code debates). HER-INDUSTRY-MIRROR bridges this divide with realistic AI multi-agent sandboxes, gamified escape rooms, and verifiable AI skill passports.

## 🚀 How to Run and View the Presentation

### Method 1: Double-Click (Fastest & Simplest)
1. Open File Explorer and navigate to `C:\Users\win\HER-INDUSTRY-MIRROR`.
2. Double-click **`index.html`**.
3. It will open directly in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave).

### Method 2: Python Local Server (Optional)
If you prefer running via a local web server:
```powershell
cd C:\Users\win\HER-INDUSTRY-MIRROR
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8000`

## 📊 The 11-Slide Deck Outline

1. **Title & Vision**: Introducing *HER-INDUSTRY-MIRROR* and Ananya's journey from CS student to industry leader.
2. **The Problem**: The confidence & experience divide, imposter phenomenon, and the leaky career pipeline.
3. **Who is Affected**: Centered on **Ananya (Third-year Computer Science student)**, alongside career switchers, relaunchers, and Tier-2/3 talent.
4. **Our Proposed Solution**: The AI Flight Simulator model for women in tech.
5. **How the Solution Works**: The 6-Stage Evidence Pipeline (`LEARN` ➔ `PRACTISE` ➔ `DEMONSTRATE` ➔ `AI FEEDBACK` ➔ `IMPROVE` ➔ `BUILD EVIDENCE`).
6. **Technology Used**: Multi-agent Generative LLMs, Web Speech API, RAG Case Bank, and Verifiable Credentials.
7. **FEATURE 4 — Real-World Escape Room (The WOW Feature)**: **“DAY 1 — YOUR FIRST DAY AT A TECH COMPANY”** (Interactive simulation through 9:30 AM unclear requirement, 11:00 AM database outage, 2:00 PM teammate disagreement, 4:00 PM manager update, ending with the Workplace Readiness Report & unlocking *Managing Conflicting Requirements*).
8. **FEATURE 5 — AI Skill Passport (The Evidence Layer)**: Visual side-by-side comparison between **Traditional Certificate ("What I completed")** vs. **AI Skill Passport ("What I demonstrated" - SQL Applied Level, Retail Analytics, Workplace Simulation)**.
9. **FEATURE 6 — Women Career Growth & Support Ecosystem**: Built on `EXPOSURE + PREPARATION + MENTORSHIP + SAFE PRACTICE = CAREER GROWTH`. Tackles unwritten workplace navigation scenarios without deficit framing.
10. **Meet The Team**: Multi-disciplinary showcase with roles, skills, and social links.
11. **Conclusion & Call to Action**: Ananya's empowerment wrap-up: *“When women practice the future, they own the present.”*

## 🏆 Hackathon Pitch Tips for Presenting Ananya's Journey

1. **Narrate Through Ananya**: Judges connect with human stories. Introduce Ananya on Slide 1 & 3, step into her shoes during Day 1 on Slide 7, and show her verified Passport on Slide 8.
2. **Interact Live on Slide 7**: Click through the 9:30 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, and 4:00 PM buttons to demonstrate the live AI feedback in front of the judges!
3. **Highlight the Evidence Distinction on Slide 8**: Emphasize that traditional certificates only prove passive course completion, while HER-INDUSTRY-MIRROR provides verifiable proof of demonstrated performance under stress.
4. **Reinforce the Empowering Stance on Slide 9**: State clearly that women do not lack talent—they deserve equal exposure, safe rehearsal, and executive mentorship.
